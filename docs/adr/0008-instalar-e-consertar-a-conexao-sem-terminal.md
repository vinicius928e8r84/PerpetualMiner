# The Conexão com o Anki installs without a terminal and mends itself, or mends with one button

The way Boas-vindas tells the person to link the Conexão com o Anki (put the Conexão in the Anki as a file, then restart the Anki) was written as provisional. The owner stated what the lasting version has to be, in their own words: use this for now, but there has to be an installer when I hand it to other people, frictionless and stable, so that when the installer fails the person can fix it easily, or does not even need to know it failed, or fixes it with a simple button.

That became three requirements for every path that installs or recovers the Conexão: (1) it installs with no terminal; (2) when it fails it mends itself; (3) or it mends with one button. The product already follows them where it can: Consertar sozinho closes an Anki that stopped answering for 30 s and opens it again, or turns on a Conexão that was disabled and restarts the Anki (ADR 0019); a Conexão that vanished from the Anki gets an Instalar de novo button; a repair that failed offers Baixar diagnóstico. A new install or recovery flow is held to the same three.

The installer itself was placed outside the redesign, in the later effort of an installable Conexão. When it exists, the install step of Boas-vindas and the instructions with Detalhes in the Conexão area are replaced by its button.
