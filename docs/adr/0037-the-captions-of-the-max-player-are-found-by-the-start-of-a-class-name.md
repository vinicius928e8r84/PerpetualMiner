# The captions of the Max player are found by the start of a class name

The Max player writes the title, the season and episode and the episode name in `Title-`, `SeasonEpisode-` and `Subtitle-Fuse-Web-Play…`; that "Subtitle" is the subtitle of the title, not the caption of the video, and a hidden label repeats the speech only for screen readers. The prefix stays while the styled-components hash changes, so a class is matched at the start of a token (`EpisodeTitle-Fuse-…` is not `Title-Fuse-…`) and never by the whole name. The speech on screen is the `TextCue-Fuse-Web-Play…` elements: a cue under a hidden ancestor (an old track, captions off) does not count, a cue that wraps another cue is left out so the speech does not come twice, and sibling lines of a two-line speech are joined. On Max the reading stops there: the generic fallback would match the episode name.
