# Arquivos da extensão

O papel de cada arquivo da raiz, de `lib/` e de `veio/`, com os testes de Node e os arquivos que andam com eles. Lido sob demanda, não em toda sessão.

A regra `indice-de-arquivos` (`scripts/regras/indice-de-arquivos.js`) exige uma linha, aqui ou em [testes-e-ferramentas.md](testes-e-ferramentas.md), para cada arquivo novo da raiz, de `lib/`, de `veio/` e do topo de `scripts/`, e acusa a linha que cita um arquivo que não existe mais. Estas duas tabelas usam `merge=union` no `.gitattributes` (duas inserções no mesmo ponto não conflitam); por isso a mesma regra acusa duas linhas com a mesma primeira coluna no mesmo arquivo, a duplicata que o `union` deixaria passar.

| `bookreader/bookreader.html/js/css` | Minerar de uma foto (ex-BookReader): book photos → Gemini Vision reads the marked passages → the person picks which phrases to mine. See “Minerar de uma foto” in the `minerar-foto` skill |

| `lib/configuracao.js` | `PerpetualMiner.Configuracao` — the first-run setup in one place: the 9 steps of the Boas-vindas, when each is done, “agora não”, `pm_boas_vindas`, and the “Configuração pela metade” reminder (ONB-14, ONB-15). | `lib/mineracao.js` | `PerpetualMiner.Mineracao` and `PerpetualMiner.minerarFraseManual` — shared mining requests and selection context. See the module's doc comments for the API, confirmation and title-compatibility contracts |

Writers move to it (`gravacao-de-frases`); `testes/test_historico.js` interleaves a photo save with a panel edit; `e2e/tests/historico-trancado.spec.js` proves the lock in Chromium. | `lib/apagar-frases-pendente.js`, `lib/envio-gravado.js` | Resends a pending Apagar tudo every 30 s; `EnvioGravado` writes `pm_envio` |
| `lib/pedido-ao-anki.js` | `PerpetualMiner.PedidoAoAnki` — the only way the extension asks the Conexão com o Anki: one builder per request, and nothing here hands out a raw `Response` of a private route. `enviarSemAssinatura(endereco, pedido, opcoes)` is only for `lib/pareamento.js`, which sends its own unsigned requests with it (`saude` and the pairing; any other request throws). `e2e/variantes-do-pedido-ao-anki.js` lists every request for `node testes/test_contrato_fechado.js`. The same file holds `PerpetualMiner.ConversaComOAnki`, the one way back: `pedir(pedido, { prazoMs, formato })` sends only what a builder made, signed by `lib/pareamento.js` (except `saude` and the pairing), and always resolves to the outcome, never a `Response` and never a rejection (only a request no builder made rejects): `{ ok: true, status, dados }`, or `{ ok: false, motivo, codigo, status, dados, detalhes }` with `motivo` one of `sem_resposta` (`codigo`: `prazo`, `sem_prova`, `rede`), `antiga`, `anki_ocupado`, `a_parear`, `recusado` (`codigo`: the contract's `error`) and `invalida` (a 2xx outside the format); `detalhes` is the technical cause, ready for the Detalhes. Every request has a deadline (15 s; 20 s on the voice routes, above the 15 s the Conexão gives Google and the OpenAI, so the Conexão is the one that says the voice did not come; `prazoMs` says otherwise) and the answer is read once; `criar({ endereco, transporte, sinalDoPrazo })` swaps the transport and the clock (`node testes/test_conversa_com_o_anki.js`). `ConversaComOAnki.ENDERECO` is the Conexão's address for the pages (`ConexaoAnki.ENDERECO` is the same value, so this file loads before `lib/conexao-anki.js`); it needs nothing but this file, so `pedir` also works in the service worker, which has no address of its own. A browser that is not paired, or a Conexão from before the pairing, gets the outcome the Conexão would give (`401 pairing_required`, `404 not_found`) without the request leaving. Loaded by the service worker, the panel and the popup |

| `lib/abridor.js` | `PerpetualMiner.Abridor` — the Conexão's opener seen from the extension (#147): the host `com.perpetualminer.abridor`, the optional `nativeMessaging`, `conferirOAnki()`, `abrirOAnki()`, `consertarOAnki()` (#152) and the step's “Agora não” (`pm_abridor`). Results and the hidden `abridor/ponte.html` frame: the header comment. Loaded by the panel and the popup |
| `lib/abrir-o-anki.js` | `PerpetualMiner.AbrirOAnki` — opening the Anki on the person's click (#150) and the opener's own repair (#152, BRG-39): `abrirOuConsertar()` (`consertar` first, `abrir` only for a closed Anki), `esperarOAnki()`, the Abrir o Anki button (BRG-38). Loaded by the panel and the popup |

| `lib/palavra-automatica.js` | `PerpetualMiner.PalavraAutomatica` — the choice of the Palavra for the Mineração automática and for saving a phrase, as a pure function: `escolher(texto, lang, vocabulario, { limit, requireVocabulary })`, `avaliar(texto, lang, vocabulario)` (accept or skip, with the reason), `escolherCom` (the Vocabulário may be a function, called only when the text has words). Loaded by the service worker |
