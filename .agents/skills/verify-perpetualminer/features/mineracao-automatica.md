# Automatic mining and the toolbar badge

With the automatic mining on for a language (Inglês, Russo), the extension mines by itself the caption of a playing video in which the person knows everything but one thing (an exact I+1), up to a limit per day. What the Vocabulário says is known: a word or Termo that is Recente, Maduro or Misto in the deck; a word that is only Novo (the Cartão was never studied) or is not in the deck is unknown. Grammar words (`the`, `of`, `и`, `не`…) and names in the middle of a sentence count as known. A caption with nothing new, or with two or more new things, is not mined; when the code cannot be sure, it asks a cheap AI (`auto-ia`). The Nível of the Idioma (the one in the Ajustes de envio) also guides it: the Iniciante only gets short captions and common words. The person sees the count in three places that must agree: the popup tile (`12 de 20 hoje`), the counter on the floating button of the page (`12/20`) and the badge of the toolbar icon (the sum of the languages that are on). At midnight the day turns and the counters start again.

## Sub-features

- `auto-settings` the popup's **Mineração automática por dia**: a tile per language with the switch (`Mineração automática em inglês`), the limit field (`Limite por dia em russo`, 1 to 100: `150` becomes `100`, `-5` becomes `1`; Enter saves and keeps the focus; clicking selects the number) and the counter (`12/20` on screen, `12 de 20 hoje` for the screen reader). Saving writes `pm_auto_miner_settings`; a failed write puts the switch back and POP-16 asks to try again.
- `auto-limit` at the limit the field gives way to `Limite de hoje atingido` (POP-08, also the switch's description) and nothing more is mined; the floating button says `Mineração automática · Russo: limite de hoje atingido`.
- `auto-mining` a playing video (not paused) with a caption mines it, adds one to the count (`pm_auto_miner_daily`), shows the pill `Mineração automática` and updates the popup and the page even if the diagnostic write (`pm_auto_miner_last_event`) fails.
- `auto-rule` the caption enters only if exactly one word is left after taking out what the Vocabulário knows, the grammar words and the names: that word is the Palavra grifada of the Frase (a verb like `fritter` with its particle, even with `it` in between: `fritter away`). Novo is unknown (it enters), Recente, Maduro and Misto are known (`nada_novo`, not mined); two or more unknown words are `nao_e_i_mais_1`, in English and in Russian; with no Vocabulário (Anki closed and no earlier reading) nothing enters (`sem_vocabulario`). The reason of the last evaluated caption is `reason` in `pm_auto_miner_last_event`.
- `auto-level` the Nível of the Idioma, read again for every caption (changing it in the Ajustes de envio counts from the next caption, no reload). **Iniciante**: while the Vocabulário of the Idioma has fewer than 200 known words (Recente, Maduro, Misto or seen; Novo does not count) only a caption of up to 3 words is looked at; a longer one is `linha_longa`, before anything else is worked out. From 200 known words on, the size is the normal ceiling (`too_long_en` above 240 characters in English). The one new word must be on the frequent-word list of the Idioma (`lib/palavras-frequentes-en.js` and `-ru.js`, 1500 words each); otherwise `fora_do_nivel`. `I love you` with `love` unknown enters (`keywords: ['love@2']`). **Intermediário** and **Avançado**: today's ceiling and any new word. The default Nível is Iniciante, so a spec that mines a long caption or an uncommon word seeds `ankiBridgePresets: { en: { nivel: 'intermediario' } }`.
- `auto-ia` the AI is asked only when the code is in doubt, after the free filters (language on, day limit, text quality, length, Vocabulário): when nothing is left after the elimination (the known words together may form a phrasal verb, an expression or a slang the person does not know) or when two unknown words are close (at most 2 words between them: they may be one Termo). The extension asks the Conexão (`POST /ia/termos`, deadline 5 s) for the Termos of the line; a Termo of several words counts as one thing, known only if the Termo itself is Recente, Maduro or Misto in the Vocabulário (knowing its loose words is not enough), and a name counts as known. `She can't put up with him` with every word known and `put up with` out of the Vocabulário enters with `put up with` as the Palavra grifada; with `put up with` Recente it is `nada_novo`; `Tell Walter I'm here` is `nada_novo` and `Walter` is not marked. The AI is never asked for the Iniciante (the default Nível, from the Ajustes de envio of the language), for one unknown word, for three or more, for two far apart, for a one-word line, for a line over 500 letters or for a caption the free filters refuse; the same line is asked once (the answer is kept in the service worker by the text, up to 200 lines). Who pays follows the AI of the cards: the ChatGPT plan when signed in, the OpenAI key when not, and with neither (or the Conexão off, or the deadline over, or a broken answer) the code decides alone by the loose words and the video does not wait. `pm_auto_miner_last_event.evaluation.iaPerguntada` says whether the AI was asked.
- `auto-day-turn` the stored count of yesterday is zero today, before any alarm; the `pm-virada-do-dia` alarm resets an open popup and wakes up an open tab that was at the limit, which mines the caption on screen and counts `1` of today, without reloading and without rewriting yesterday's diary.
- `auto-badge` the toolbar badge sums only the languages that are on (`12`, tooltip `Mineração automática: 12 hoje`); both off: no badge and the tooltip `PerpetualMiner`; all the ones that are on at their limit: the sum with `Mineração automática: limite de hoje atingido`; one at the limit and another not: the counter. Turning a language on/off in the popup and each automatic mining change it at once; the midnight alarm fixes a stale badge and schedules itself for the next local midnight; the colours are the Veio tokens.
- `auto-idle` with both languages off the content script does not read the page for captions (it only looks for the video, for the floating button); with a language on it reads the caption on every round, and with both on the button's counter is the caption's language.
- `auto-max` on Max the automatic mining saves the caption on screen and never the episode name: [legenda-da-max.md](./legenda-da-max.md).

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Click the toolbar icon › **Mineração automática por dia**: turn a language on, set the limit.
- Play a video with a caption in that language on any page; the counter shows on the floating button over the player.
- Read the badge on the toolbar icon (hover for the tooltip).

## Driving it with the Playwright e2e harness

Preconditions: `doctor.sh` green. The specs seed `pm_auto_miner_settings` and `pm_auto_miner_daily` (the *starting* state) and, for the accept rule, a cached deck vocabulary `pm_auto_keyword_vocab_ru` with no known word. The fixture player (`player.html`) is given a real playing video by a canvas stream; `tocarVideo(page)` does it for the Max page.

- **Popup tiles (`auto-settings`, `auto-limit`).** `drive.sh auto-popup popup.spec -g "azulejos Inglês|Enter no limite|limite de hoje atingido|contador mostra"`. Observable: `12 de 20 hoje` and `0 de 15 hoje`, the English switch checked and the Russian one not; ligar Russo stores `pm_auto_miner_settings.ru.enabled: true`, `30` stores `dailyLimit: 30`, `150` and `-5` store `100` and `1`; at `20/20` no limit field and `Limite de hoje atingido`. Failure and loading states: `-g "mineração automática carregando"`, `-g "sem o estado do service worker"`, `-g "gravar a mineração automática falhando"`.
- **Badge (`auto-badge`).** `drive.sh auto-badge badge-da-toolbar.spec`. The spec reads `chrome.action.getBadgeText/getTitle` in the service worker. Observable: `{ badge: '12', tooltip: 'Mineração automática: 12 hoje' }` with the Russian 13 left out; `{ badge: '', tooltip: 'PerpetualMiner' }` with both off; `25` plus the limit tooltip; yesterday's day gives `0`; the alarm test replaces `pm-virada-do-dia` with one that fires in 100 ms (what Chrome would do at midnight) and the next alarm is local `00:00` tomorrow with no period. `-g "cada mineração automática soma um"` mines a Russian caption and the badge goes from `4` to `5`. `-g "a vitrine mostra"` and `-g "cores da identidade Veio"` compare with `veio/tokens.css`.
- **Day turn (`auto-day-turn`).** `drive.sh auto-virada mineracao-automatica-virada.spec`. Evidence: `virada-aba-antes.png`, `virada-aba-depois.png`, `virada-antes.json`, `virada-depois.json`. Observable: the tab at `3/3` mines nothing (`frases()` empty), then with yesterday's date and the alarm fired `frases()` has exactly the Russian caption, the counter `1/3` and the stored daily `{ date: <today>, en: { count: 0 }, ru: { count: 1 } }`.
- **Diagnostic write failing (`auto-mining`).** `drive.sh auto-publicacao mineracao-automatica-publicacao.spec`. The spec makes the service worker's `chrome.storage.local.set` throw for the `pm_auto_miner_last_event` write; observable: popup and tab still go from `2/3` to `3/3`.
- **Off means idle (`auto-idle`).** `drive.sh auto-parada overlay-sem-varredura.spec`. The spec enters the content script's isolated world through the Chromium protocol and records the selectors asked of `querySelectorAll`. Observable: with both languages off only `video` is asked in two rounds; with both on the counter says `0/20` (the English caption's limit).
- **The I+1 rule (`auto-rule`).** `drive.sh auto-regra mineracao-automatica-i-mais-1.spec`. The spec gives the fake Anki a vocabulary (`fakes.anki.definirVocabulario('en', …)`), plays the caption `The ferry left the harbour.` and reads the stored state. Observable: with `ferry` and `left` known and `harbour` Novo the Frase is saved with `keywords: ['harbour@8']` and `pm_auto_miner_last_event` `{ action: 'saved', reason: 'en_new_item' }`; with `harbour` Recente `{ action: 'skip', reason: 'nada_novo' }` and no Frase; with only `ferry` known `{ action: 'skip', reason: 'nao_e_i_mais_1' }`; the daily count only goes up on the saved one.
- **The Nível (`auto-level`).** `drive.sh auto-nivel mineracao-automatica-iniciante.spec`. The spec seeds `ankiBridgePresets` with the Nível and the fake Anki with a vocabulary, plays a caption and reads the stored state. Observable: Iniciante with an empty vocabulary and `I love you` saves it with `keywords: ['love@2']`; with `old`, `man`, `left` known and `house` Novo, `The old man left the house` is `{ action: 'skip', reason: 'linha_longa' }`, and with 200 known words it is saved (`keywords: ['house@10']`); `I see zebra` is `fora_do_nivel`; Intermediário and Avançado save `The old man left the zebra`; after `ankiBridgePresets` changes to Intermediário the next caption is saved in the same tab.
- **The AI in doubt (`auto-ia`).** `drive.sh auto-ia mineracao-automatica-termos.spec`. The spec gives the fake Anki a Vocabulário and a fake key (`fakes.anki.espelharChave`), sets the Nível in `ankiBridgePresets`, plays the caption and reads the stored state plus the requests the fake Anki received at `/ia/termos`. Observable: `She can't put up with him` at Intermediário saves the Frase with `keywords: ['put@4', 'up@6', 'with@8']` and `evaluation.iaPerguntada: true` after one request; with `put up with` Recente `{ action: 'skip', reason: 'nada_novo' }`; `Tell Walter I'm here` at Avançado `nada_novo` with no keyword; at Iniciante no request at all and `iaPerguntada: false`; with no key in the Conexão (`409`) the line is `nada_novo` decided by the code, after one request.
- **The accept rule without a browser.** `node scripts/testar.mjs node` runs `testes/test_palavra_automatica_tabelas.js` (the table of lines, Vocabulário and the outcome), `testes/test_palavra_automatica_iniciante.js` (the Iniciante's line and word), `testes/test_palavra_automatica_termos.js` (the table of doubt cases with the Termos of the AI), `testes/test_mineracao_automatica.js` (the decision with fake doors, the Nível included, counting the asks), `testes/test_termos_da_linha.js` (the ask, its check and the memory by line) and `testes/test_auto_keyword.js`.

## Gotchas

- The automatic mining only looks at a video that is playing: a paused video or one without a stream mines nothing. The accept rule needs the deck vocabulary, and the fake Anki starts with an empty one (every word unknown, so a caption with more than one word is not mined until the spec gives it a vocabulary); with no cached reading the popup tile says `— esperando o Anki` ([anki-baralhos.md](./anki-baralhos.md) › `anki-data-source`); the seal `Vocabulário de ontem, 14:02` (and the tile's `— esperando o Anki`) with the Conexão off is `popup.spec.js -g "o selo do popup|Conexão desligada"`, where the fake Anki decides which reading exists.
- The toolbar badge and alarms are native Chrome: the specs read them through the service worker and fire the alarm by creating one with the same name, so say "simulated alarm" in the proof; the real midnight is not waited for.
- Dates are local days (`YYYY-MM-DD` of the browser), so a spec that crosses midnight on the host can flip; the specs derive "today" and "yesterday" from `new Date()`.
- Never mix this with the Frases `Mine a phrase` shortcuts: Alt+S is [mineracao.md](./mineracao.md).
