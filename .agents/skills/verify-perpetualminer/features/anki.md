# Anki connection and send

The panel talks to the local Anki Connection (the bridge today; the add-on in `complemento/` answers `/health`, the collection reads, the OpenAI key and the login item, and registers the opener when it loads; the bridge still does the Envio) on `127.0.0.1:8766`. The **Conexão com o Anki** area (`painel.html#anki/custos|baralhos|vocabulario|ajustes`) shows whether it is on, the costs of each send, decks and vocabulary, and the send settings. **Enviar ao Anki** in the Frases bar sends the selected phrases (one Envio per language) with a progress modal and a summary when it ends.

## Sub-features

- `anki-status` the sidebar dot and the F1 strip say Conectando / ligada / desligada; Tentar de novo rechecks; a silent port counts as off after 3 s.
- `anki-custos` the Custos tab: period, tiles per language, list × detail of Envios, partial Envio with Continuar envio.
- `anki-offline` with the Conexão off the last data stays with the BRG-12 note (`Anki fechado. Mostrando dados de {quando}.`); Continuar envio and Reparar Anki are unavailable with a tooltip that says why.
- `anki-send-progress` Enviar ao Anki opens the progress modal at once, one card per language Envio, no toast.
- `anki-send-summary` when Envios finish, the summary (`Envio concluído` / `… em parte`) opens in any area: `+N cartões`, the deck, "já estava lá", "não entrou", Tirar do histórico / Manter.
- `anki-send-retry` Tentar de novo / Continuar envio resumes one Envio.
- `anki-first-connection` ONB-16: the "Ainda não houve conexão neste computador" card with its four steps (the fourth is `anki-open-alone`; Ter o Anki is `anki-have-anki`; Pôr a Conexão no Anki: the warning animation, Baixar a Conexão com o Anki, the "O arquivo não abriu?" Detalhes; Reiniciar o Anki: Conectando, also on the automatic 15 s check with no click › "Achei o Anki · perfil · versão") until the first answer; two minutes without one, counted from when the steps are on screen, show the 3-item checklist and Baixar de novo. No terminal command anywhere. The Pronto moment comes once step 4 is resolved.
- `anki-have-anki` ONB-18, step 1 “Ter o Anki” (#146): the question “Você já tem o Anki?” with **Já tenho** / **Não tenho**; the choice lives only on the screen and before the Conexão the step says nothing about the Anki (never “não encontrado”). **Não tenho** opens the Anki's download page in a new tab (the panel stays on the step) and the sentence after it follows the system (`navigator.userAgentData?.platform` else the user agent): the drag to Aplicativos on the Mac, the installer on Windows, the general one elsewhere. **Já tenho** uses the opener's `abrir` (`Abridor.abrirOAnki`) when the permission is already granted (`O Anki está abrindo…`, then the 15 s check finds the Conexão the opened Anki brought); without the permission, without the opener, without the Anki in place or with the Anki in place and not opening (`{ achou: true, abriu: false }`, the contract's `nao_abre`), the manual path (“Abra o Anki para continuar.”). The ONB-19 warning animation lives in the download step: two frames alternating by themselves, still and side by side with reduced motion.
- `anki-open-alone` ONB-17, step 4 “Abrir o Anki sozinho” (#147): right after the first answer the card opens it; **Permitir abrir o Anki** asks Chrome for the optional `nativeMessaging` and the extension checks the opener the Conexão registered (only “is Anki there?”: `O Anki abre sozinho quando um Envio precisar.`, the step ✓); with the permission already granted it checks without a click; **Agora não** stores `pm_abridor: { agoraNao: true }` and marks the step “agora não”; not found / no answer (10 s) use a persistent Veio aviso with Tentar de novo and the technical cause in Detalhes. The permission wait survives reload (`pm_anki_abridor_pendente`) until found or Agora não; its title is “Abrir o Anki sozinho” after HTTP health succeeds, including a subsequent health drop and reload while the opener remains pending. Pronto keeps the opener's outcome visible. The same step is in Boas-vindas step 4.
- `anki-settings` Ajustes de envio: default deck per language, saved only on Salvar ajustes.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Sidebar › **Conexão com o Anki** (Custos, Baralhos e termos, Vocabulário, Ajustes de envio tabs).
- In **Frases**: tick phrases › **Enviar ao Anki** in the selection bar.
- Boas-vindas step 4 (recipe `bv-steps3-6` in [boas-vindas](./boas-vindas.md)).

## Driving it with the Playwright e2e harness

Preconditions:

- `doctor.sh` green. The Anki fake is a stateful queue behind the same HTTP contract as `bridge/server.py`. The specs freeze the clock and call `fakes.anki.usarRelogio(() => AGORA)`.
- The tests, not a human, play the bridge: `fakes.anki.envios` lists created Envios, `moverEnvio(jobId, { stage, progress })` advances one, `concluirEnvio(jobId, { duplicadas, falhas })` ends it.

- **Send and summary (`anki-send-progress`, `anki-send-summary`).** Run `drive.sh anki-envio painel-envio.spec -g "as duplicatas do Anki aparecem"`. The spec seeds 3 English phrases, ticks `Selecionar frase` on each, clicks `Enviar ao Anki` in `#frases-barra`, waits for the fake to hold one Envio, then `concluirEnvio(job.jobId, { duplicadas: [LATE.id] })`. Observable: dialog `Envio concluído` with `3 frases · 1 baralho`, group `Inglês` containing `+2 cartões`, `no baralho CIMV`, `1 já estava lá` and no "não entr"; `Tirar do histórico` focused. Also run `-g "Enviar ao Anki abre o progresso na hora"` for the progress modal (dialog `Enviando ao Anki…`, `Envio em inglês · 2 frases` and `Envio em russo · 1 frase`, and the Envio the fake received: `lang 'en'`, entries in order, line `sack : She hit the sack early. [Contexto: netflix - Desconhecido - Desconhecido]`).
- **Keep or remove (`anki-send-summary`).** `drive.sh anki-manter painel-envio.spec -g "Manter marca as enviadas"`. Observable: after `Manter`, `frases()` has `status: 'exported'` on exactly the sent and already-there phrases; the failed one has no status; no toast; the bar still says `4 selecionadas`.
- **Status (`anki-status`).** Run `drive.sh anki-status conexao-anki.spec -g "com a porta muda"`. The spec turns the fake off, opens `painel.html#anki/custos` (sidebar dot `data-estado="desligada"`), turns it on but silent (`fakes.anki.silenciar()`), clicks `Tentar de novo`. Observable: the dot goes `conectando`, then `desligada` within 3 s, then `ligada` after `voltarAFalar()` and another `Tentar de novo`, with the F1 strip hidden. Evidence: `painel-tentando-conectar.png`, `painel-conexao-ligada.png`.
- **Offline with last data (`anki-offline`).** `drive.sh anki-offline painel-anki.spec -g "cai depois de responder"`. Observable: F1 text `A conexão com o Anki está desligada. Ligue-a neste computador e tente de novo.`, `Anki fechado. Mostrando dados de há 2 h.` (BRG-12, the `anki` form; the catalog also has an `atualizacao` form, `Mostrando dados de {quando}. Não deu para atualizar agora.`, this recipe does not show), the 4 Envios still listed, `Continuar envio` with `aria-disabled="true"` and its description; after `fakes.anki.ligar()` + `Atualizar` the Reparar Anki button is enabled again.
- **Custos tiles (`anki-custos`).** `drive.sh anki-custos painel-anki.spec -g "azulejos por idioma"`. Observable: one tile per language with total and Envios; picking one filters the summary, images and list.
- **Custos period (`anki-custos`).** `drive.sh anki-periodo painel-anki.spec -g "o período abre em Este mês"`. Observable: `Este mês` checked and the fake's last costs request asks `from=2026-09-01&to=2026-09-18`; `Hoje` asks `from=2026-09-18&to=2026-09-18` and lists 3 Envios; filling `De` with `2026-07-01` asks `from=2026-07-01` and unchecks `Hoje`.
- **Custos list × detail, Continuar envio (`anki-custos`).** `drive.sh anki-detalhe painel-anki.spec -g "lista × detalhe"`. Observable: the first Envio selected, `Envio em inglês · 6 frases` with `há 10 min`; picking `Envio em russo` shows `Concluído em parte · 3 de 4 frases` and `1 não entrou`, `Detalhes` shows `collection is not available`, and `Continuar envio` shows `Envio retomado` and makes exactly one `POST /jobs/job-ru-parte/retry` reach the fake.
- **Retry one Envio (`anki-send-retry`).** `drive.sh anki-retry painel-envio.spec -g "Tentar de novo no bloco do idioma"`. Observable: only that language's Envio is retried and the next summary counts what became a card.
- **Settings (`anki-settings`).** `drive.sh anki-ajustes painel-ajustes.spec -g "mexer no formulário não grava nada"`. Observable: editing writes nothing; `Salvar ajustes` stores only the open language and shows toast BRG-07.
- **First connection (`anki-first-connection`).** `drive.sh anki-primeira painel-anki-primeira-conexao.spec`. Observable: the first-connection card replaces the tabs until the fake answers `/health`, then the Pronto moment. `-g "Baixar a Conexão com o Anki entrega"` clicks the link and checks the downloaded `perpetualminer.ankiaddon` is the file in `conexao/`; `-g "dois minutos sem resposta"` runs the page clock 2 min and shows the checklist (the fake never opens Anki, so what happens when the person opens the file in a real Anki is the owner's rehearsal).
- **Have the Anki (`anki-have-anki`).** `drive.sh ter-o-anki ter-o-anki.spec`. `-g "o primeiro passo é Ter o Anki"`: the question and the two answers, nothing about the Anki on screen. `-g "no Mac"` and `-g "no Windows"` fake `navigator.userAgent`/`userAgentData` (`abrirComo`) and check the download-page link (new tab) and the after-download sentence per system; `-g "fora do Mac e do Windows"` clicks it and the panel stays on the step. `-g "sem a permissão do abridor"`: the manual path, the opener got nothing. `-g "mesma tela"`: not installed, closed and open without the Conexão give the same screen, and the opener is never asked. With `test.use({ permissaoDoAbridor: true })`: `-g "o abridor abre o Anki"` shows `O Anki está abrindo…`, `pedidos()` starts with `{ acao: 'abrir' }`, and after `fakes.anki.ligar()` + a 15 s clock run the first three steps are ✓ and the card is on the opener step; `-g "o abridor não acha o Anki"` (`fakes.abridor.definir('nao_achei')`), `-g "sem conseguir abri-lo"` (`'achei_sem_abrir'`) and `-g "sem o abridor no computador"` fall back to the manual path; `-g "duas vezes"`: a double click sends one `abrir`. `-g "a animação mostra o aviso"`: two frames, the Sim marked in the second, and with `emulateMedia({ reducedMotion: 'reduce' })` (`-g "menos movimento"`) both frames still and side by side; `-g "janela estreita"`: below 560 px they stack inside the card. Evidence: `ter-o-anki-pergunta.png`, `ter-o-anki-mac.png`, `ter-o-anki-windows.png`, `ter-o-anki-animacao.png`, `ter-o-anki-animacao-parada.png`, `ter-o-anki-abridor-abriu.png`.
- **Open the Anki alone (`anki-open-alone`).** `drive.sh anki-abridor abrir-o-anki-sozinho.spec`. The fixture registers a fake opener in each test's Chromium profile (`fakes.abridor`: `definir('achei'|'nao_achei'|'achei_sem_abrir'|'sem_resposta')`, `desregistrar()`, `pedidos()`), answering from `complemento/contrato/exemplos.json` › `abridor`. `-g "Agora não guarda"`: the step's name ends `agora não`, storage has `pm_abridor: { agoraNao: true }`, the opener got nothing. `-g "o passo confere o abridor sozinho"` (`test.use({ permissaoDoAbridor: true })`): `pedidos()` is exactly `[{ mensagem: { acao: 'conferir' }, origem: 'chrome-extension://ohpoogiccjmdgilgegholdidgfhjkadf/' }]`, four steps ✓, then Pronto with the result visible. `-g "recarregar retoma"`: reload before choosing, the step is still visible and `pm_anki_abridor_pendente: true`; Agora não then clears the wait and a second reload keeps the tabs. `-g "confirmada mantém o título"`: after first health, no choice, turn the fake off, Atualizar, reload; the step opens on Reiniciar o Anki (“Conexão com o Anki: desligada” and Tentar de novo), the card title stays “Abrir o Anki sozinho” and never returns to “Ainda não houve conexão”, and both `pm_anki_ja_conectou` and `pm_anki_abridor_pendente` stay true. `-g "disse Agora não, com a Conexão desligada"`: someone who connected before and said Agora não, with the Conexão off, gets step 4 of the Boas-vindas on Reiniciar o Anki with Tentar de novo. `-g "o abridor não acha|sem o abridor registrado"`: the Veio aviso and its Details/action are visible. `-g "Permitir pede a permissão"` only shows the waiting line (`Responda ao aviso do Chrome.`): Chrome's prompt is native and Playwright cannot answer it, so the granted path comes from the fixture seeding the profile the way Chrome stores Permitir. Evidence: `abridor-passo-3.png`, `abridor-achou-pronto.png`, `abridor-nao-achou.png`, `abridor-agora-nao-pronto.png`, `abridor-boas-vindas-achou.png`.
- **Proof.** `painel-anki.spec.js` calls `fotografar` in some tests (`c1-…` files); `painel-envio.spec.js` does not. For a send, the strongest evidence is the assertions on `fakes.anki.envios` plus a `fotografar(page, 'anki-envio-resumo.png')` you add in a scratch copy right after the summary appears.

## Gotchas

- This proves the panel against the *contract*, not the bridge or Anki. A green run does not prove the real send. Say so; the bridge side is `bridge/test_envio.py`.
- The bridge's real port is 8766 and the fake never opens it. If `doctor.sh` notes a listener there, it is not part of the run.
- Double-clicking Enviar ao Anki must create one Envio per language, not two (`bug #9: duplo clique`); count `fakes.anki.envios`.
- With the Conexão off, Enviar ao Anki is unavailable and its tooltip explains; do not try to force it.
- The summary waits while another modal is open and opens on its own after a reload (`recarregar com um Envio em andamento`).
- The Windows "could not close Anki" Envio shows its own instruction with Continuar envio (`um Envio parado porque o Windows`).
- `anki-open-alone` proves the extension against the fake opener. The real script the Conexão writes (`.sh` on the Mac, `.bat` + `.ps1` on Windows) and its registration are `python3.9 complemento/test_abridor.py` (temp folders and fake registry only; the Mac shell script runs, with its JXA JavaScript executed via Node on Linux, not real osascript); whether Chrome finds it on the Mac and whether the Mac opens Anki without a question are the owner's rehearsal; on Windows, Chrome 153 and Opera GX 136 found the host in HKCU's Chrome key (`complemento/LEIA-ME.md`, risk 1 verified 2026-10-10), and the panel steps on a real Windows are still unproven (port 8766 held by the bridge). Answering the real Chrome prompt was checked once by hand under Xvfb (keyboard Tab + Enter on “Permitir”), not in the suite.
- Native Messaging fixtures are Linux-shaped: the host manifest goes in `<perfil>/NativeMessagingHosts` and the host is a `#!/bin/sh` script.
