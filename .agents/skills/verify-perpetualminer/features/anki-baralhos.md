# Baralhos e termos, Vocabulário and the Conexão on screen

The Baralhos e termos and Vocabulário tabs of the **Conexão com o Anki** area show what the Conexão reads from the Anki collection: the decks of each language with their maturity, and the vocabulary (words and terms) of each deck. The same Conexão state (`ligada`, `desligada`, `Conectando`) is said in one way on every screen that shows it. The area, the Custos tab and the Envio are in [anki.md](./anki.md).

## Sub-features

- `anki-decks` Baralhos e termos: one tile per language with the deck (`EN · PerpetualMiner::Inglês`, `Inglês`, `3.412 palavras`), the chosen one pressed (`aria-pressed`) with the maturity ring (`66% de maturidade`: mature over all cards) and the numbers Cartões, Termos-alvo, Palavra nova por cartão, Maduros, Recentes (learning + young), Novos. A deck that is not in the Anki shows `—` and `baralho não encontrado` on the tile, BRG-14 and **Abrir Ajustes de envio**; loading is BRG-10 (skeleton), a failed read BRG-11 with **Tentar de novo** and the cause in Detalhes (no Reparar Anki). Entering the area reads the two decks of Ajustes de envio in one request and never asks the Conexão to open the Anki.
- `anki-vocabulary` Vocabulário: tile per language (`5 itens`), list × detail (`termo · 4 cartões`, `Misto`/`Maduro`; the detail has Aparece em, Maduros, Recentes, Novos, Status), arrows/Home/End choose, Tipo, Status and the search filter (`5 itens em inglês · mostrando 2`); no result is BRG-21 with **Limpar busca** (not offered when the language has no item at all); loading BRG-19, a failed read BRG-20 with Tentar de novo; a failed Baralhos read does not hold the Vocabulário back (#9).
- `anki-data-source` what the two tabs say about where the numbers came from, with the Conexão on: the Anki open reads from it and says nothing; the Anki closed (or open without the AnkiConnect) reads the collection file: BRG-13 on Baralhos (`Números lidos direto do arquivo do Anki. Para enviar, o Anki precisa abrir normalmente.`) and, only with the Anki closed, `Anki fechado. Mostrando dados de há 2 h.` (the age of the data, renewed by **↻ Atualizar**, which asks the Conexão to open the Anki); the Conexão off keeps the last data with BRG-12; with no reading at all the tiles say `— esperando o Anki`. A reading of another Baralho does not count after the deck is changed in Ajustes de envio.
- `anki-connection-shared` the Conexão's state is said once (`lib/conexao-na-tela.js`) and the F1 strip, the ONB-16 card and Boas-vindas step 4 show the same text (`Conexão com o Anki: desligada|ligada`, `Conectando ao Anki…`) and dot; **Tentar de novo** is `aria-disabled` (never `disabled`) while it checks, keeps the focus and does not ask twice; when the Conexão turns on, the focus goes to a real element, never the `<body>`.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Sidebar › **Conexão com o Anki** › **Baralhos e termos** (`painel.html#anki/baralhos`) or **Vocabulário** (`painel.html#anki/vocabulario`); **↻ Atualizar** reads again.
- The Conexão's state: the sidebar dot and the F1 strip, the first-connection card (ONB-16) and step 4 of the Boas-vindas ([anki.md](./anki.md) › `anki-first-connection`).

## Driving it with the Playwright e2e harness

Preconditions: `doctor.sh` green. The Conexão is the Anki fake (`fakes.anki`) answering `POST /anki/stats/decks` and `POST /anki/vocabulary` from `complemento/contrato/exemplos.json`; the specs freeze the clock (`2026-09-18T12:00:00`) and keep to it.

- **Baralhos e termos (`anki-decks`).** `drive.sh anki-baralhos painel-anki-baralhos.spec -g "azulejos Inglês · Russo"`. The spec opens `painel.html#anki/baralhos`. Observable: two tiles (`EN · PerpetualMiner::Inglês` `3.412 palavras`, `RU · PerpetualMiner::Russo` `612 palavras`), the first `aria-pressed="true"`, the ring `66% de maturidade`, Cartões `2.140`, Termos-alvo `1.870`, Maduros `1.410`, Recentes `420`, Novos `310`; clicking the second shows `Russo · PerpetualMiner::Russo` and Cartões `480`; the fake got one `POST /anki/stats/decks` with `decks: [{ deckName: 'PerpetualMiner::Inglês', lang: 'en' }, { deckName: 'PerpetualMiner::Russo', lang: 'ru' }]` and no `launch`. States: `-g "baralho que não existe"` (BRG-14, Abrir Ajustes de envio goes to `#anki/ajustes`), `-g "baralhos carregando"` (BRG-10), `-g "baralhos que falham: BRG-11"` (Detalhes `POST /anki/stats/decks · HTTP 503`, then Tentar de novo reads again).
- **Vocabulário (`anki-vocabulary`).** `drive.sh anki-vocabulario painel-anki-baralhos.spec -g "vocabulário: azulejos|vocabulário: tipo"`. Observable: 5 options in English, the first `take for granted` `termo · 4 cartões` `Misto` selected, its detail region with Maduros `3`; ArrowDown moves focus and selection, End goes to the last; `Só termos` leaves 2, `Novos` 1 (`cut corners`), the search `BAR` finds `barely`, `zzz` shows `Nenhum item com esta busca.` and **Limpar busca** brings the 5 back and focuses the field. `-g "vocabulário carregando"` (BRG-19), `-g "vocabulário que falha"` (BRG-20), `-g "idioma sem nenhum item"` (BRG-21 without Limpar busca).
- **Where the numbers came from (`anki-data-source`).** `drive.sh anki-origem painel-anki-baralhos.spec -g "cai depois de responder|Conexão ligada mas o Anki fechado|sem o AnkiConnect|Atualizar com o Anki fechado"`. Observable: the Conexão off after an answer shows `Anki fechado. Mostrando dados de há 2 h.` on both tabs with the last data and the search still working; the Conexão on with the Anki closed shows BRG-13 plus the same age and no F1; the Anki open without the AnkiConnect shows BRG-13 and not `Anki fechado`; **Atualizar** two hours later says `há 2 h`, four hours later `há 4 h`, without reloading. `-g "↻ Atualizar"`: the last `POST /anki/stats/decks` has `refresh: true` and `launch: true`, the vocabulary grows to 6 and `pm_anki_vocabulario` keeps the new reading. Against the contract's own collection: `-g "coleção do contrato"`.
- **The Conexão said the same everywhere (`anki-connection-shared`).** `drive.sh anki-na-tela conexao-na-tela.spec`. Observable: off (`Conexão com o Anki: desligada`) and connecting (`Conectando ao Anki…`), the sidebar, the ONB-16 card and step 4 of the Boas-vindas say the same text and carry the same `data-estado`; while a `/health` is held, Tentar de novo is `aria-disabled` without `disabled`, keeps the focus, and pressing it again adds no `/health` request; when the fake turns on, the button leaves and the focus lands on the chosen tab (F1, `Custos`) or on `#bv-titulo` (step 4). Individual titles: `-g "desligada: a sidebar"`, `-g "conectando: as três superfícies"`, `-g "F1: fica aria-disabled"`, `-g "o botão sai e o foco vai"`.

## Gotchas

- Baralhos e termos and Vocabulário prove the screens against the contract (`fakes.anki.falharBaralhos`, `lerBaralhosDoArquivo`, `fecharOAnki`, `ankiAbertoSemAnkiConnect`, `definirVocabulario` and the others in `e2e/fakes/anki.js` play the Conexão); what the real Anki holds in its collection is the add-on's adapter (`complemento/test_colecao_em_memoria.py`, `test_leitura_dos_arquivos.py`, `test_servidor_leitura.py`) and the owner's rehearsal. The specs freeze the clock; "há 2 h" is `page.clock.setFixedTime` two hours later, not waiting.
